🫙 What is Turkish Turşu — and why is it different?
🛢️ Pakistani Oil Achar
  • Preserved in mustard oil
  • Heavy masala & spices
  • High calorie, oily texture
  • Stains hands & clothes
  • Can feel heavy on stomach
  • Sometimes has artificial color
🥒 Turkish Turşu
  • Preserved in vinegar & brine
  • Clean, tangy, crunchy taste
  • Zero oil — much lighter
  • No staining at all
  • Easy on digestion
  • Natural — no artificial additives
💡 Fun fact: In Turkey, people drink the pickle juice (turşu suyu) as a health tonic after meals. It's rich in natural probiotics — similar to how our elders used to drink lassi for digestion!
